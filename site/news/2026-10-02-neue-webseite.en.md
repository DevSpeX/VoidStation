# VoidStation has its own website

VoidStation now has a home at **voidstation.de** – in the start screen's tile design, of course.

Here you'll find the download, an overview of the [features](seite:features), [help](seite:help) with controls and frequently asked questions, and all the news. Every version released in the Stable channel shows up in the news automatically – along with the list of what's new. Subscribe to the [RSS feed](/en/news/feed.xml) if you like.

> Update from October 4, 2026: the live ISO is here, and it is now the only way to install VoidStation – find it on the [download page](seite:download).
