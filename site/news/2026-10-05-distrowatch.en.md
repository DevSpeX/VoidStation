# VoidStation is on the DistroWatch waiting list

As of today, VoidStation is on the [DistroWatch waiting list](https://distrowatch.com/new) – the big directory of Linux and BSD distributions. One day after the first live ISO, that's a nice next step.

What it means: new distributions don't go straight into the DistroWatch database, they go on a waiting list first. They usually stay there for about a year before being evaluated – many new projects fade away during that time. VoidStation intends to show it's here to stay: with regular releases, answers to questions and a live ISO that runs on as many machines as possible.

## How you can help {#help}

- **Recommend it:** DistroWatch evaluates projects with many recommendations first. If you like VoidStation, recommend it on the [waiting list](https://distrowatch.com/new) – once is enough.
- **Try it and tell us:** Does the [live ISO](seite:download) run on your machine? Show off your setup or ask your questions in the [Discussions on GitHub](https://github.com/Panther92/VoidStation/discussions). Old office PCs getting a second life are especially welcome.
- **Report bugs:** If something doesn't work, an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Panther92/VoidStation/issues) helps most. What's useful to include is listed under [Reporting bugs](seite:contribute#bugs).

Thanks to everyone who has already tried VoidStation!
